Questions

What is a bio website?

A one-page website with your photo, a short line about you and buttons to all your important links: reels, videos, shop, bookings, WhatsApp. You put its address in your Instagram or YouTube bio.

How much does it cost?

₹500 for the bio design made with your details. If you also want us to connect your own domain name and put it online for you, tell us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the price.

Which platform do the designs use?

Every bio design is made for Blogger only, Google's free website platform. It is not for WordPress, Wix or any other platform. You need a free Google account and a Blogger blog (we can set it up with you). There is no hosting bill, you can change your text, links and photo yourself from the Blogger dashboard, and your own domain name can be connected to it.

Is what I type on this site saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so you can come back to it. Nothing is sent to us until you press "Get this bio site" and send the WhatsApp message yourself.

Can I change my links later?

Yes. On Blogger you can edit them yourself at any time, or send us the new links on WhatsApp.

Are the sample people real?

No. Every name, handle and link in the samples is made up.

Ready to see yours?

It takes two minutes. Nothing is sent until you choose.
